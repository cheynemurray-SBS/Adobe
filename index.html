Good to know

Already have a personal Adobe account? It's separate from your Redlands account. Projects don't move between them automatically, so download anything you want to keep and re-upload it while signed in with your Redlands email.
Stuck in a sign-in loop? Sign out of Adobe completely, clear your browser cookies for adobe.com (or try a private window), then sign in again with your @redlands.edu address.
Using it for class? Instructors can share templates and assignments through Express. Share your finished work as a link, or download it as PNG, JPG, PDF, or MP4 to submit in Canvas.
Generative AI features (Generate image, Generate template, and similar) use Adobe's monthly generative credits. Follow your instructor's AI policy for each assignment.

How to request Creative Cloud Pro

Send one email to the IT Help Desk from your Redlands account:

tech_support@redlands.edu

  1. Email the Help Desk

    Send your request from your @redlands.edu email. Include the course or project and the apps you need.

  2. Watch for confirmation

    IT will reply once your Creative Cloud Pro license is assigned to your Redlands account.

  3. Install Creative Cloud

    Download the Creative Cloud desktop app on your Windows or Mac computer.

  4. Sign in and install apps

    Sign in with your Redlands email, choose Company or School Account if asked, then click Install on the apps you need.

What to include

  • Your full name and Redlands email
  • Your student ID number
  • The course name and number, or the project you're working on
  • Which apps you need, if you know

Not sure which apps you need? Use the project finder above, or just describe your project and the Help Desk can help.

Common questions

Which one do I actually need?

Start with Adobe Express. It covers graphics, social posts, flyers, simple video, presentations, and everyday PDF tasks. Request Creative Cloud Pro if your course requires a specific professional app — for example Photoshop for photo retouching, Premiere Pro for video editing, InDesign for layout, or Acrobat Pro for advanced PDF work.

Do I need to request Adobe Express?

No. Adobe Express is available to every Redlands student. Just sign in with your @redlands.edu email.

Does the Pro suite cost anything?

There is no charge to students for a university-assigned license. Don't buy a personal subscription for a class before checking with the Help Desk.

Can I install the Pro apps on my own laptop?

Yes, once your license is assigned. Install the Creative Cloud desktop app on your Windows or Mac computer and sign in with your Redlands email. Check Adobe's system requirements for demanding apps like Premiere Pro and After Effects.

What happens to my access when I graduate or leave?

Both Express and Pro are tied to your active Redlands account. Before you leave, download or export any projects and files you want to keep.

Where can I get help?

Contact the IT Help Desk at tech_support@redlands.edu (tech_support@redlands.edu). For learning the apps, Adobe's free tutorials are available inside each app and at helpx.adobe.com.
